Participating residues are the union of reference-sequence contact sites within a mode. Default: ≥4 residues (strictly more than 3). Mode ID = original pocket_id + 1. Results describe the entire structure set. “Query chain observed” indicates whether the queried chain contributed a contact record to that mode. The representative chain is the receptor from the mode's Jaccard medoid contact; it may differ from the reference chain. Reference positions are not PDB residue numbers.
